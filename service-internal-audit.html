Internal Audit & Readiness

Independent internal audits and pre-audit readiness checks, so nothing surprises you when the certification body arrives.

This covers two related needs — the internal audit that ISO 27001 requires, and a final readiness check before your external audit. Both are about finding the gaps before an auditor does.

Two complementary offerings

Internal Audit
ISO 27001 requires your ISMS to be independently audited before certification and at intervals after. For companies whose ISMS we didn't build, Stenward conducts that internal audit — execution against the standard, findings documented and classified, a clear report, and remediation recommendations.
Pre-Audit Readiness Check
Built your own ISMS, often on a platform, and want senior eyes before the real audit? We review your documentation, Statement of Applicability, and evidence, identify the findings an auditor is likely to raise, and hand you a prioritised fix-list — so you walk into your audit confident.

Note: For clients whose ISMS we build, we coordinate the internal audit through a trusted independent partner — the standard requires independence, so we can't audit our own work.

Both are short, focused engagements designed to de-risk your certification.
