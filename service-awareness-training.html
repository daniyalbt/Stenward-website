Security Awareness Training

Security and data-protection awareness training your team will actually engage with — and that auditors look for.

ISO 27001 expects your people to be trained in security awareness, and auditors look for evidence of it. But generic, box-ticking training doesn't change behaviour. Stenward builds awareness training that's tailored to your business, covers the data-protection angle properly, and deploys through tools you already use.

What's covered

Core security awareness aligned to ISO 27001 expectations
Data-protection and GDPR awareness (a particular Stenward strength)
Role-relevant content rather than one-size-fits-all
Deployable through your existing LMS or tools
Refreshable each year to stay current for surveillance audits

How you can get it

Security awareness training is included as part of a Stenward ISO 27001 implementation. If you've been certified elsewhere and want to add tailored training, it's available as a standalone module. And if you want to refresh annually to stay current for your surveillance audits, we can help with that too.

Strong awareness training is one of the simplest ways to reduce real risk — not just to pass an audit. When your team understands why security matters and what they're expected to do, compliance becomes something they build into their work, not something you have to police.
