Gap Analysis

A clear, honest picture of where you stand against ISO 27001 — and exactly what it will take to get certified.

Before committing to certification, it's worth knowing the real distance to the finish line. A Stenward gap analysis gives you a straight answer: what you already have, what's missing, and what the path looks like — with no obligation to go further.

What you get

A review of your existing documentation, policies, and controls
Stakeholder interviews across security, engineering, operations, and HR
A full assessment against ISO 27001 management-system requirements and Annex A controls
A prioritised remediation roadmap with effort estimates
A written report you can act on or take to leadership
A follow-up call to talk it through

When it makes sense

A gap analysis is useful before committing to a full implementation, as a sanity check at any stage, or when leadership wants a clear-eyed view before allocating budget.

Good news: If you go on to a full implementation with us, part of the gap-analysis fee is credited towards it.
