Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Introduction

Stenward ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise handle your information when you visit our website and interact with our services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, company name, job title, and phone number when you book a call or contact us
  • Communication Data: Information you provide in emails or messages to us
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited, time spent, and referring URLs

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Schedule and conduct consultation calls
  • Send you relevant updates about our services (with your consent)
  • Analyze website usage to improve our services and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses third-party services including:

  • Cal.com: For booking consultation calls. Cal.com's privacy policy governs the data you provide through their booking widget

These third parties have their own privacy policies governing their use of your information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request that we restrict how we use your information

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@stenward.com.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. When you request deletion or when information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
